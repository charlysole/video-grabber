Everything between a link and a file

  • Paste links, get files

    Line up as many links as you want. Up to ten download at the same time, each with its progress, speed and time left.

  • Pick the quality

    Take the best available, or choose the exact resolution and format. Save audio only as MP3.

  • Convert, trim, add subtitles

    Convert to MP4, MOV or MKV, keep just a part of the video, and download subtitles in English or Spanish.

  • Record live streams

    Watch and record HLS, RTSP and RTMP streams, or YouTube and Twitch live, and schedule recordings ahead of time.

  • Downloads that recover

    When a site cuts a download short, it retries on its own. The download engine, yt-dlp, updates itself so site changes don't break things.

  • Free and open source

    No ads, no account, no tracking. The code is public under the MIT license.

Finds the video on any page

Open a page in the built-in browser and Video Grabber lists the videos it plays, even on sites no downloader supports.

Signed in somewhere? Downloads can use your cookies from the built-in browser, Firefox, Chrome, Brave or Edge, so videos behind a login work too.

Make it fit

Need a video under 25 MB for an email or a chat? Right-click one video, or several, and compress them to the size you choose.

Fast mode uses your Mac's hardware encoder and is several times quicker. Precise mode takes longer and lands closer to the size.

Install in a minute

  1. Download the .dmg from the button at the top of this page.
  2. Drag it to Applications in the window that opens.
  3. Open Video Grabber It's notarized by Apple, so it opens without warnings.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Video Grabber is free and open source under the MIT license. There are no ads, no account and no paid version.

Which sites does it work with?

Every site supported by yt-dlp, more than 1,000, including YouTube, Vimeo, Instagram, TikTok, X, Facebook and Twitch. For other sites, the built-in browser can often find the video the page plays.

Does it work on Intel Macs?

No. The current version needs a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 14.6 or newer.

Is it safe to install?

Yes. It's signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, which checks it for malware. The source code is public on GitHub.

Can I download any video?

That depends on the video and on the laws where you live. Download videos you have the right to save: your own, public domain works, or content with a license that allows it, like the Creative Commons films in these screenshots.
